Core gains their canvas and pointer motion, the changelog and docs-reference logic and the book CSS. Light mode E is the light theme (`--primary-ink` for small oxide text, `--primary-display` in headings). Buttons get more side padding (sm px-3, md px-4, lg px-5). MaterialCard has one layout, words on glass over the material; the cover, side and frame variants are gone.
