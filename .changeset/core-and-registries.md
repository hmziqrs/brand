First release of the brand as installable pieces: @hmziq/brand-core on npm, and both kits' registries (`/r/svelte/`, `/r/astro/`) with every piece in the roster, the vendored stock components with the brand's changes, and the `kit` item that installs everything. `pnpm new-project` starts a site from either boilerplate with the kit installed from its registry (docs/kits.md step 3).
