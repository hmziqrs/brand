# Writing

These sites are for people, not only for developers. The words should make sense to someone who has never opened a terminal.

## Rules

1. **Say what it does for them.** Lead with the outcome, not how it's built.
2. **Plain words first.** If there's a technical term, the plain version comes before it, or replaces it.
3. **One idea per sentence.** Short sentences, active voice.
4. **Buttons say what happens.** "Subscribe", "Download for Mac", "Read the blog". Not "Submit" or "Learn more".
5. **No terminal cosplay on the first screen.** No fake boot logs, version numbers or `$ commands` in the hero.
6. **No hype.** Skip "blazing", "next-gen", "revolutionary". Say the real thing.
7. **Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it.** No apologies, no codes on their own.

## Technical detail still has a place

Docs, changelogs and READMEs can be as technical as they need to be. The rule is about the first thing a visitor reads: the headline, the intro and the buttons.
